RHINO AND PIP
Series 1 — sixteen chapter scripts
5 September 2026

Working scripts. We can still move things.
Rhino and Pip are original. The stories are original.
Short-page friendship stories inspired the rhythm.
No names, likenesses, lines, or plots from other series.


================================================================
HOW EVERY CHAPTER WORKS
================================================================

They are already playing.
The child is already there.
One joke per chapter.
The child taps.
Right: one reaction.
Wrong: match what they actually tapped.
If a tap can be wrong in more than one way, classify the square.
Do not play a dark-square line on a light-square miss.
If there is only one way to be wrong, pick 1 of 3 at random.
The joke can change. The fact cannot.
After two or three misses, use the PLAIN LINE.
On the fourth miss in a row, use SHOW.
Pip lands on the answer by accident.
Rhino:  That is the one.
The tap counts as done. Go on.

When the taps are done, they celebrate.
The celebration is silly and separate.
It is not a prize for being good.
It is what friends do when a game is over.

Nobody says chess until Chapter 16.

CHAPTER 1 — THE FANCY CORNER
================================================================

Quietly holding: the floor is two colours. The right-hand corner is light.
The joke: Pip is sure dark is fancier.
Taps: 4.
Celebration: The Very Tall Ice Cream.


OPEN

The floor is already there. No pieces.
Pip stands on the near-left corner. Dark.
Rhino stands on the near-right corner. Light.
They look at the child.

Pip:    This corner is the good one.
Rhino:  Why.
Pip:    Dark is fancier.
Rhino:  I thought the one on the right was light.
Pip:    That cannot be right.
         Dark has mystery.
         Dark has style.
Rhino:  We should ask.

Pip:    Oh.
Rhino:  Light on the right.
Pip:    It is a little fancy.
Rhino:  A little.
Pip:    I was still mostly right.
Rhino:  You were standing.

WRONG — classified. Match the square, top to bottom.
a1 — Pip's corner:
   Pip:    See. They like mine.
   Rhino:  They tapped yours. Yours is dark.
   Pip:    Dark is still winning.
   Rhino:  Dark is not the corner we need. The right one.
h8 — the far light corner:
   Rhino:  That is a corner. That is light.
   Pip:    Then we are done.
   Rhino:  That one is far. We want the near one.
   Pip:    Near is a feeling.
   Rhino:  Near is by your hand.
any light square:
   Rhino:  That one is light.
   Pip:    Light is what we wanted.
   Rhino:  Light in a corner.
   Pip:    Corners are so far away.
   Rhino:  This one is by your right hand.
any dark square:
   Pip:    Another one of mine.
   Rhino:  That one is dark.
   Pip:    I have many.
